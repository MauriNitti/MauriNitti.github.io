Adunata — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Adunata is a virtual tabletop application published by Maurizio Nitti ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal data Adunata processes, why, and what rights you have over it.

The short version: Adunata runs no servers of its own, contains no analytics or advertising, and collects nothing for marketing. The only data processed is what is technically necessary to sign you in and connect you to the other players at your table.

2. What Adunata processes, and why

Data Why
Steam ID and Steam display name To sign you in and to show your name to the other players in your session. Read from the Steam client on your computer when Adunata starts.
Epic Online Services Product User ID The account identifier Adunata uses for online play. It identifies you to Epic's services and to the other players' copies of Adunata so they can connect to you.
Epic Account ID Only if you choose to sign in with an Epic Games account instead of Steam.
An anonymous device identifier A fallback sign-in method, used only when neither Steam nor an Epic account is available. It is not linked to any name or email address.
IP address and network connection details Necessary to establish a connection between the players in a session. Handled by Epic Online Services; see section 4.
Campaign data you save to the cloud If you host a campaign, Adunata can back it up so you can restore it later. See section 3.
Content you share during a session Maps, images, audio, tokens and journal notes you share are sent to the other players in that session so they can see them. See section 5.

Legal basis. We process the data above to perform our contract with you — without it, online play does not work (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Where we process data to keep sessions stable and prevent abuse, we rely on our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).

What Adunata does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No crash or diagnostic reports are sent to us. Log files stay on your computer.
  • We never sell or rent personal data, and we do not use it for marketing.
  • We do not operate servers that store your data. Everything described here is handled either on your own computer or by Steam and Epic.

3. Cloud campaign backups

When you host a campaign, Adunata can store a copy of that campaign — its index and its scene files — using Epic Online Services Player Data Storage, associated with your Epic Product User ID. This exists so that you can recover a campaign, or carry it to another computer.

These files contain the campaign content you created, which may include images, notes and other material you imported. We have no ability to browse this storage across users; it is keyed to your own account.

To have your cloud-stored campaign data deleted, contact us at the address in section 1 and we will action it. You can also delete campaigns from within Adunata.

4. Third parties

Adunata relies on two external providers. Each acts as an independent controller for the data it handles, under its own privacy policy:

  • Valve Corporation (Steam) — sign-in, ownership verification and your display name. Steam Privacy Policy
  • Epic Games, Inc. (Epic Online Services) — account identifiers, peer-to-peer connections between players, and cloud campaign backups. Epic Games Privacy Policy

Adunata does not use Unity Gaming Services, and sends no data to Unity Technologies.

International transfers. Both providers are established in the United States, so data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area. Those transfers take place under the safeguards each provider maintains, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. Content you share with other players

Adunata ships with no third-party game content. Maps, images, audio, documents and notes are supplied by you.

When you share that material in a session, it is transmitted directly to the other players taking part and stored on their computers so that it can be displayed. This is how the application works, and it means you should only share material you are entitled to share. We have no access to that content and no way to recall it once it has been shared.

If you believe material shared through Adunata infringes your rights, contact us at the address in section 1.

6. Data stored on your own computer

Adunata keeps your saves, imported audio, cached images and log files locally on your computer. This data is not transmitted to us. You can remove it by deleting Adunata's data folder or by uninstalling the application.

7. How long data is kept

Account identifiers are retained by Steam and Epic for as long as your accounts with them exist, under their own policies. Cloud campaign backups remain until you delete them or ask us to delete them. Data on your own computer stays until you delete it.

8. Children

Adunata is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly process the data of anyone under 16 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). Steam and Epic accounts have their own minimum age requirements.

9. Your rights

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable form. To exercise any of these, write to us at the address in section 1 — we will respond within one month.

Because most of the data described here is held by Steam and Epic rather than by us, requests about your account itself are often best directed to them. We will help you do so if you ask.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how Adunata handles data, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the application or on its store page.
